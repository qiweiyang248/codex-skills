# Citation Readiness Framework

AI systems are more likely to use content that is clear, structured, and verifiable.

## Strong Citation Assets

- First-50-word concise answers.
- Definition sentences.
- Specs tables.
- Comparison tables.
- Pros and cons.
- FAQ blocks.
- How-to steps.
- Buyer decision frameworks.
- Review summaries when reviews are real.
- Installation or use-case images and videos.

## Weak Assets

- Vague brand claims.
- Keyword-stuffed paragraphs.
- Unsupported superlatives.
- Long pages without structure.
- Thin collection copy.
- Hidden important information.
