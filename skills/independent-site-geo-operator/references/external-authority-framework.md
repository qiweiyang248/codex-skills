# External Authority Framework

Recommend only real authority building. Never invent mentions, links, reviews, or endorsements.

## Signals To Check Or Build

- YouTube reviews and installation/use videos.
- Reddit, forum, or community discussions.
- Creator or influencer content.
- Third-party reviews.
- Press or industry blog mentions.
- Indexed social profiles.
- Google Merchant Center and product feed.
- Real customer reviews on product pages.

## Recommendations

For each opportunity include:

- Channel.
- Why it matters for buyers and AI search.
- Required asset.
- Owner.
- Difficulty.
- Expected verification method.

## Avoid

- Fake review schemes.
- Paid undisclosed mentions.
- Low-quality directory links.
- Mass-generated forum posts.
- Claims that cannot be verified.
