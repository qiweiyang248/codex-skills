# Conversion Review Checklist

Use this checklist when SEO recommendations touch product, collection, or homepage copy.

## Above The Fold

- Can a first-time buyer tell what is sold within 3 seconds?
- Is the target buyer or use case clear?
- Is the differentiator specific?
- Is the CTA visible and understandable?
- Does the main image explain the product value?

## Product Page

- Product bullets are specific and scannable.
- Specs answer size, fit, material, compatibility, installation, care, and warranty questions.
- Reviews, ratings, shipping, returns, and warranty are visible where available.
- FAQ answers likely objections before purchase.
- Mobile purchase path is short.

## Collection Page

- Intro explains how to choose, not only what the category is.
- Filters are understandable.
- Related collections and guides help shoppers continue.

## Output

Include conversion implications in SEO recommendations when the same fix improves search and buyer decision clarity.
