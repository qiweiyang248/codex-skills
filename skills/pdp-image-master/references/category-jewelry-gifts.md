# Category Reference: Jewelry, Keepsakes, Premium Gifts

Use this reference for rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings, watches, pendants, keepsake boxes, engraved gifts, sentimental products, and premium gift-oriented small items.

## Buyer Questions

- Who is it for and what emotion does it carry?
- How does it look worn, held, or gifted?
- What size/scale should the buyer expect?
- What material, finish, stone, engraving, clasp, chain, or packaging proof matters?
- Is it suitable for the occasion?

## Required PDP Emphasis

- Emotion/gift identity image is highly important.
- Worn/hand/neck/wrist/ear/packaging context often reduces uncertainty.
- Detail macro is required for small items.
- Show gift box/package only if included or clearly labeled as styling prop.

## Claim Rules

Do not invent:

- Gold/silver/stainless/titanium material
- Karat, plating thickness, gemstone type, diamond, moissanite, crystal
- Hypoallergenic / nickel-free / tarnish-resistant
- Handmade
- Waterproof
- Personalization options
- Gift packaging included

Use material or stone names only when supported.

## Image Sequence Adjustments

1. Main macro hero with clear silhouette and finish.
2. Gift/emotion/occasion image: anniversary, birthday, memorial, friendship, partner, family, etc.
3. Buyer task: meaningful gift, everyday elegance, personal keepsake, outfit finishing touch.
4. Real-use trust: worn on hand/neck/wrist/ear, held, opened box, natural light.
5. Function: clasp, adjustable chain, engraving, opening/closing, personalization view if supported.
6. Detail proof: stone, engraving, finish, edge, chain, clasp, setting.
7. Scale/fit: hand, wrist, neck, ear, ruler if provided, common small object.
8. Specs/package: dimensions, chain length, ring size, included items, care, confirmed material.
