# Category Reference: Beverage-Contact, Drinkware, Barware

Use this reference for products that touch beverages or are used around drinking, pouring, serving, chilling, storing, opening, decanting, or displaying drinks.

Examples:

- Glasses, cups, mugs, bottles, tumblers, straws, decanters, drink dispensers
- Whiskey/wine/bar accessories, bottle openers, stoppers, ice molds, cocktail tools
- Coffee/tea vessels and beverage storage items

## Buyer Questions

Answer these questions visually:

- Is it safe or appropriate for beverage contact?
- What does it look like with liquid or in a real serving scene?
- How large is it compared with a hand, glass, bottle, shelf, or table setting?
- Does pouring, opening, sealing, gripping, or cleaning look easy?
- Does it feel premium enough for display, gifting, or hosting?

## Required PDP Emphasis

Include at least two real-use trust signals when possible:

- Hand-held product
- Pouring close-up
- Cap/removal/seal detail
- Product on a lived-in bar, coffee table, kitchen counter, or dining table
- Natural reflections, glass distortion, wood grain, condensation, water marks, liquid motion, or human-scale context

Keep realism premium and controlled, not messy.

## Claim Rules

Do not invent or exaggerate:

- Lead-free
- BPA-free
- Food-grade
- Borosilicate glass
- Hand-blown
- Airtight
- Leakproof
- Dishwasher-safe
- Heat-resistant
- Cold-resistant
- 750ml or other capacity
- Holds a full bottle
- Crystal / handmade / mouth-blown / laboratory-grade

Use these only when provided by spreadsheet, supplier notes, certificate, packaging, product parameters, or user approval.

## Conservative Wording Map

Only use these mappings when the source explicitly contains the left-side evidence.

- `高硼硅玻璃` → `Borosilicate Glass`
- `抛光` → `Polished Finish`
- `吹制` → `Blown Craftsmanship`

Do not upgrade `吹制` to `Hand-blown` unless the source explicitly says handmade, hand-blown, mouth-blown, or equivalent.

## Image Sequence Adjustments

For beverage-contact products, adapt the universal 8-image sequence as follows:

1. Main product hero with clear glass/metal/shape identity.
2. Gift/hosting/lifestyle identity image.
3. Buyer task: elevate bar setup, serve cleanly, store/display beverage, or make gifting easier.
4. Real-use trust: hand, pouring, liquid, bar/kitchen counter, or table scene.
5. Function: pour, open, seal, chill, mix, hold, clean, or serve.
6. Material/craft/detail proof: close-up edge, cap, stopper, surface, glass thickness, finish, or tool mechanism.
7. Scale reference: hand, glass, bottle, shelf, cup holder, fridge, cabinet, or table setting.
8. Specs/package/trust: confirmed capacity, dimensions, included items, care instructions, verified material.

## Visual Style Tips

- Use believable reflections and refraction for glass.
- Avoid impossible liquid behavior.
- Avoid making glass too perfect or plastic-like.
- Keep labels and engravings legible if present.
- Do not include alcohol-specific claims if the product is generic drinkware unless the product context supports it.
- Avoid implying alcohol is included unless it is part of the actual product.
