# Ecommerce Growth Principles

## Foundation

SEO lets Google and Bing crawl, understand, and rank the site.

GEO lets AI answer engines understand, cite, compare, and recommend the brand or products.

Conversion work turns visibility into revenue.

## Best Fixes

Prefer fixes that improve all three:

- Clear product title and H1.
- Specific above-the-fold value.
- Structured product specs.
- FAQ that answers objections.
- Product and Organization schema.
- Internal links from guides to products and collections.
- Collection intro copy that helps shoppers choose.
- Real trust signals and policy clarity.

## Avoid

- Generic SEO reports without executable assets.
- Unsupported claims.
- Fake authority or reviews.
- Low-quality AI search long-tail pages.
- Direct live edits without explicit approval.
