# llms.txt Guidance

Use `llms.txt` cautiously.

## Principles

- It can be an auxiliary discovery and orientation signal for some AI systems.
- It is not required for Google AI Overviews or Google AI Mode ranking.
- It does not replace crawlability, indexing, useful content, internal links, or schema.
- It must not expose private, gated, or unsafe content.

## Recommended Contents

- Site name and short description.
- Canonical important pages.
- Product category pages.
- Buying guides and FAQ hubs.
- Policy pages.
- Contact/about pages.

## Output

If recommending `llms.txt`, provide:

- Proposed file path.
- Draft content.
- Risk level.
- Rollback method.
- Verification method.
