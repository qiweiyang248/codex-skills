# AI Search Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to judge whether AI answer engines can understand, cite, and recommend the site.

## Crawl Access

- Important product, brand, collection, and guide pages are crawlable.
- `robots.txt` does not accidentally block major AI crawlers.
- Sitemap and internal links expose priority pages.

## Entity Clarity

- Brand name and category are clear.
- About page explains who the brand is and what it sells.
- Organization schema and sameAs links are present when real.
- Contact, shipping, returns, warranty, and policy pages are easy to find.

## Product Entity

- Product name, model, size, color, material, function, use case, installation, specs, price, availability, warranty, and certifications are clear where relevant.

## Answer Structure

- Pages include concise answer blocks, specs tables, comparison logic, FAQs, and how-to steps when appropriate.

## Authority Signals

- Real third-party mentions, creator content, forum discussions, reviews, social proof, Google Merchant Center/product feed, and indexed social profiles are identified or planned.

## Output

Each issue must include Page URL, Page Type, Issue Type, Current Value, Suggested Value, Target Field, Modification Method, Priority, Risk Level, and Verification Method.
