# GEO Scoring Model

Score 0-100.

## Weighted Score

- 20% AI Crawl Access
- 20% Entity Clarity
- 20% Answer Structure
- 15% Product Data Completeness
- 15% Trust and Authority Signals
- 10% AI Query Coverage

## Component Scoring

Use 0-5 for each component, then convert to weighted 100.

- 0: missing or harmful.
- 1: present but weak.
- 2: basic coverage.
- 3: usable but incomplete.
- 4: strong and mostly complete.
- 5: excellent, specific, and verifiable.

## Additional Scores

- Citability Score
- Brand Entity Score
- Product Entity Score
- AI Crawler Access Score
- Query Coverage Score
- External Authority Score

## Priority

Use `Impact x Confidence x Ease`.

- P0: entity or access blockers.
- P1: missing structures on core pages.
- P2: content expansion and authority building.
