# Priority Scoring

Use `Priority Score = Impact x Confidence x Ease`.

## Inputs

- Impact: 1-5. SEO, GEO, or conversion business impact.
- Confidence: 1-5. How certain the diagnosis is.
- Ease: 1-5. Higher means easier to implement.

## Priority Bands

- P0: immediate. Crawl/index, canonical, main conversion path, or core entity blockers.
- P1: within 30 days. Ranking, AI citation, core page conversion, schema eligibility, or buyer clarity.
- P2: 60-90 days. Content expansion, authority building, long-term growth.

## Tie-breaker

Prioritize items that improve SEO, GEO, and conversion together.
